# 1.1.2-nuzlocke Release Notes

`1.1.2-nuzlocke` expands the randomized world-item and direct-gift pool relative
to `1.1.1-nuzlocke`. The release focuses on making more battle strategies
available during a run without adding medicine, X-items, berries, Poké Balls,
TMs, HMs, Key Items, or money treasures to replacement results.

## Expanded useful-item pool

The equally weighted pool grows from 23 to 67 results:

| Category | 1.1.1 | 1.1.2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Evolution items | 11 | 11 |
| Held items | 12 | 56 |
| Total results | 23 | 67 |

Duplicates remain possible. No evolution item, held item, or category is
guaranteed during a run.

### Newly available held items

- The 17 previously missing single-type damage boosters, completing all 18
  types alongside Metal Coat.
- Choice Band, Choice Specs, and Choice Scarf.
- Focus Band, Muscle Band, Wise Glasses, and Expert Belt.
- Scope Lens, Wide Lens, and Zoom Lens.
- Quick Claw, Loaded Dice, Bright Powder, Covert Cloak, and Grip Claw.
- Damp Rock, Heat Rock, Smooth Rock, Icy Rock, Light Clay, and Terrain Extender.
- White Herb and Power Herb.
- Electric Seed, Grassy Seed, Misty Seed, and Psychic Seed.

The four terrain-setting moves and four Surge abilities remain eligible for
their respective move and ability randomizers, allowing the terrain seeds and
Terrain Extender to function within randomized teams.

## Randomizer version

The randomizer algorithm version increases from 4 to 5 because expanding the
pool changes deterministic world-item and direct-gift results. Existing saves
remain structurally loadable, but deterministic randomized results may differ
from `1.1.1-nuzlocke`; starting a new save is recommended for a consistent
version-5 run.

## Future berry rework

This release records, but does not yet implement, a larger berry-system rework:

- Disable planting, watering, growth, and harvesting.
- Retire the 20 berries with no held-item effect after dependency audits.
- Keep the e-Reader Enigma Berry slot out of ordinary distribution.
- Add a separate deterministic acquisition randomizer for retained
  battle-useful berries.
- Keep berries separate from the general useful-item pool and prevent renewable
  planting or harvesting rerolls.
